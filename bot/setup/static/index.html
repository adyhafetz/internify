Internify

Let's set up Internify

It takes about 10 minutes. You'll need two things: an AI key (so it can read jobs and write your resume) and a Telegram bot (so you can talk to it). Everything else is optional.

Your keys stay on this computer. They are only written into a settings file next to the project and sent to the services they belong to.

1. Choose your AI

This is what reads job postings and writes your resume.

OpenAI Best qualityPay as you go. You add a few dollars of credit and set your own limit.
Google Gemini Free, experimentalFree tier, no card. Slower limits, and on the free tier Google may use what you send to improve its products.
How do I get an OpenAI key?
  1. Open platform.openai.com/api-keys and sign in or create an account.
  2. Add credit at Billing. A few dollars lasts a long time. This is separate from a ChatGPT subscription, which does not include API use.
  3. Back on the API keys page press Create new secret key, then copy it right away. OpenAI shows it only once.
How do I get a Gemini key?
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Press Create API key and copy it.

2. Your Telegram bot

You talk to Internify through your own private bot.

How do I make a bot?
  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather (the one with the blue tick), or open t.me/BotFather.
  2. Send /newbot. Choose a name (anything), then a username that ends in bot.
  3. BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:ABCdef…. Copy it.

Your Telegram ID

So that only you can use your bot. Open your bot in Telegram, press Start, send “hi”, then press the button.

It can't find it. Another way

Message @userinfobot in Telegram. It replies with your ID (a number). Type it above.

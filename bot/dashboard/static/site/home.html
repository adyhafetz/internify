How it works

Three steps. The first one happens once.

1

Save your experience

Send your current resume as a PDF or Word file. Internify reads it into jobs, projects and bullet points that you can edit in Telegram.

2

Send a job link

Paste a link from LinkedIn, JobStreet or any careers page. If a page blocks the bot, paste the job text instead.

3

Get the PDF and a report

See which of the job's requirements the resume shows, what it left out, and what changed from your saved lines.

What it does for your applications

Every line is checked

Each bullet point is compared with the saved line it came from. A tool, number or name that is not in your original is rejected.

One page, measured

The PDF is built and its pages are counted. If it runs over, the least relevant items go first. Your internships and jobs stay on.

A match you can verify

The score comes from the requirements the job lists and the ones your resume shows. You see the ones it misses.

Internships found for youPaid

Each day it searches LinkedIn, JobStreet, Hiredly and the web for your keywords, then writes resumes for the best matches.

A tracker that follows up

Mark each job applied, in review, interviewing, offer or rejected. Get a reminder when nobody replies. Export to Excel any time.

Your data stays yours

Export everything as one file. Delete your account, and every saved record with it, in two taps.

Made for CS and tech internships

The resume is a single-column, ATS-friendly layout with Education, Work Experience, Projects, Leadership and Technical Skills. Skills are grouped as languages, frameworks, databases, AI/ML and tools. Internify can also read your public GitHub repositories to confirm tools you really use, and add them with a real bullet point. Other fields will fit less well.

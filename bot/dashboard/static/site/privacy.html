Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Internify is open-source software that writes tailored resumes. This policy covers the copy of Internify that runs at this address, operated by the person who owns this domain for their own use ("the operator"). If you run your own copy, you are responsible for the data in your copy; this policy describes what the software does so you can see it is the same.

1. What data Internify handles

  • Your saved experience: contact details, education, jobs, projects, leadership and skills that you type in or import from your own resume file.
  • Job links and job text: the links you send, the public job posting text found on those pages, and the pages you choose for the daily job check.
  • Your Telegram identity: your Telegram user and chat ID, so the bot replies to you and, if configured, accepts messages only from you.
  • Resumes it creates: PDFs and a small history of applications (company, role, link, status, match score).
  • Activity traces: a record of each run (which step did what, job text and resume lines) kept on the server so the dashboard can replay it.
  • Google data, only if you connect Google: see section 3.

2. How the data is used, and who else receives it

It is used only to read jobs, write and check your resumes, save them, and track applications. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared for any other purpose. To do that work it is sent to these services:

  • OpenAI (API): the job text, your saved experience and the draft resume are sent to OpenAI's models to read, write and check. See OpenAI's privacy policy and its API data terms.
  • Telegram: messages and PDFs are delivered through Telegram's bot service.
  • Job pages and job boards: Internify visits the job pages you send and the career pages you choose. If a page blocks automated visitors it may send only that public job link to a page-rendering service (Jina Reader), a scraping service (ScrapingAnt, if the operator set it up), the Internet Archive or a search engine (DuckDuckGo) to find a readable copy. No personal data or resume content is sent to these.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets: only if you connect your account (section 3).

3. Google user data

If you connect a Google account, Internify asks for two permissions:

  • Google Drive: to upload the resume PDFs it creates into the folder you choose. It does not read, list, change or delete your other files. The uploaded files are private to your account; Internify does not make them public.
  • Google Sheets: to add a row for each application, and update that row, in the tracker spreadsheet you choose. It reads that spreadsheet's header row and the rows it needs to find an application; it does not open your other spreadsheets.

Google data is used only to provide these two features. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not read by people except where you share it or the law requires it.

Limited Use: Internify's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your saved experience, application history, PDFs and run traces are files and a small database on the computer or server where Internify runs. Google access tokens and API keys are in the operator's private configuration file on that server and are never published. The live dashboard, which can show job text and resume lines, is protected by a secret key, and the connection to this site is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so keep your server and keys safe.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. You can edit or delete saved experience in the bot (/profile), delete resumes and rows in your own Drive and Sheet, ask the operator to delete the server's copies, and withdraw Internify's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all further use of your Google data.

6. Children

Internify is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their data.

1. What data Internify handles

  • Your saved experience: contact details, education, jobs, projects, leadership and skills that you type in or import from your own resume file.
  • Job links and job text: the links you send and the public job posting text found on those pages.
  • Pages for the daily job check: the list of career pages and job boards you choose (or that are added from jobs you sent), when each was last visited, and which jobs have already been seen or matched.
  • Your Telegram identity: your Telegram user and chat ID, so the bot replies to you and, if configured, accepts messages only from you.
  • Resumes it creates: PDFs and a small history of applications (company, role, link, status, match score).
  • Activity traces: a record of each run (which step did what, job text and resume lines) kept on the server so the dashboard can replay it.
  • Google data, only if you connect Google: see section 3.

2. How the data is used, and who else receives it

It is used only to read jobs, write and check your resumes, save them, and track applications. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared for any other purpose. To do that work it is sent to these services:

  • OpenAI (API): the job text, your saved experience and the draft resume are sent to OpenAI's models to read, write and check. The public text of career pages you chose for the daily check is also sent, so the AI can pick out the job openings and compare them with your experience. See OpenAI's privacy policy and its API data terms.
  • Telegram: messages and PDFs are delivered through Telegram's bot service.
  • Job pages and job boards: Internify visits the job pages you send, and once a day (if the daily check is on) the career pages and job boards you chose. If a page blocks automated visitors it may send only that public job link to a page-rendering service (Jina Reader), a scraping service (ScrapingAnt, if the operator set it up), the Internet Archive or a search engine (DuckDuckGo) to find a readable copy. No personal data or resume content is sent to these.
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets: only if you connect your account (section 3).

3. Google user data

{% if marketing %}

If you connect a Google account (in Telegram: /profile, then Connect Google), Internify asks for one permission: to see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (the drive.file permission). With it, Internify:

  • creates a folder "Internify resumes" and a spreadsheet "Internify — Application Tracker" in your own Google Drive;
  • uploads the resume PDFs it writes into that folder, and adds or updates one row per application in that sheet. It also reads the Status column, so a change you make in the sheet is followed.

It cannot see or open any other file in your Drive. The files use your own Google storage and stay yours if you disconnect or delete your account. Your Google login is stored encrypted on the server, and is used only for these two features. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not read by people except where you share it or the law requires it. You can disconnect at any time in Telegram (/profile, Google, Disconnect), which also asks Google to revoke the login, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

If you connect a Google account, Internify asks for two permissions:

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your saved experience, application history, PDFs and run traces are files and a small database on the computer or server where Internify runs. Google access tokens and API keys are in the operator's private configuration file on that server and are never published. The live dashboard, which can show job text and resume lines, is protected by a secret key, and the connection to this site is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so keep your server and keys safe.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. You can edit or delete saved experience in the bot (/profile), delete resumes and rows in your own Drive and Sheet, ask the operator to delete the server's copies, and withdraw Internify's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all further use of your Google data.

6. Children

Internify is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Material changes to how Google data is used will be made here before they take effect.

9. The hosted service: accounts, storage and deletion

  • Your account is created when you send /start in Telegram. It holds your Telegram ID and first name, your plan and its end date, and how many resumes and AI calls your account used this month. Nothing else about you is collected to run the service.
  • Your data is kept apart. Each account has its own folder and its own database rows. The operator runs the server and can technically access what is stored on it, and does so only to run, secure and support the service.
  • Your Google login, only if you connect Google, is stored encrypted (section 3). Deleting your account revokes it and removes it from the server.
  • Signing in on the web uses a one-time link sent to you in Telegram. The server stores only a hash of the link and of the browser session, never the values themselves.
  • Payments are made directly to the operator (bank transfer or DuitNow). Internify does not process or store card details. The operator records your plan and its end date.
  • Delete everything: send /deleteaccount in Telegram and confirm twice. Your saved experience, tracker, job pages, settings, resume files, sessions and usage are deleted at once, along with the link to your Telegram account. Files you saved in your own Google Drive stay yours.
  • Inactive accounts: if your account has had no activity for six months and has no paid plan running, you get a message in Telegram a week before it is deleted, and then its data is deleted.
  • Export: /profile → My data sends you everything in one file, so you can keep a copy or move to another copy of Internify.
  • Additional service providers: the server runs at a hosting provider, and traffic to this site passes through Cloudflare. They process data only to carry it.
  • Your rights: the operator handles personal data as the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia requires. You may ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time, using the contact below or the commands above.

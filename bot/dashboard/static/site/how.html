How it works

From a job link to a one-page resume

Internify works inside Telegram. You save your experience once. After that, each job link takes one message.

Set up once

  1. Open the bot and send /start. This creates your account.
  2. Send your current resume. A PDF, Word or text file. Internify shows what it found. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
  3. Check it in /profile. Tap any job, project or bullet point to edit, delete or add a line.

What happens to a job link

  1. Reads the page. If the site blocks the bot, it tries other ways in, and asks you to paste the job text as a last step.
  2. Works out what the job wants. Tools, requirements and keywords, and whether it is an internship.
  3. Finds your matching experience. It ranks your saved bullet points for this job. Every job or internship you have keeps its best points.
  4. Writes the resume. It rewords your saved lines for the role, and cites the saved line each one came from.
  5. Checks every line. Code compares each bullet point with its source. A tool, number or name that is not in the source sends it back for a rewrite.
  6. Builds the PDF and counts the pages. If it is longer than one page, it shortens wording and leaves out the least relevant items.
  7. Sends the PDF and a report in Telegram: the requirements it shows, the ones it misses, and what was reworded or left out.

After you apply

  • Tap I applied, then move the job through in review, interviewing, offer or rejected.
  • If nobody replies, Internify asks you for news after two weeks, and reminds you before a closing date.
  • Export the tracker to Excel, or export all your data as one file, at any time.

On the paid plan

  • Set the jobs you want, for example "software engineer intern", and where.
  • Each day Internify searches LinkedIn, JobStreet, Hiredly and the web for them, skips closed jobs and ones you already have, and picks the best matches.
  • It writes a resume for each match and sends it to you. You decide whether to apply.

What it does not do

  • It does not apply to jobs for you.
  • It does not invent experience. If a job needs a tool you have not saved, it tells you, and offers to add it if you really have used it.
  • It does not promise an interview. You should read every resume before you send it.

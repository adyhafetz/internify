Opportunity, connected.

One connection. A world of possibility.

Internify is a personal assistant for students and job seekers. You send a job link to a private Telegram bot. It reads the job, finds the parts of your own saved experience that match, writes a one-page resume from them, checks that every line is true, and sends you the PDF. Each day it can also look at the job pages you choose and write resumes for the best matches.

Open the live dashboardPrivate. Needs a key.

How it works

  1. You send a job link in Telegram (or let the daily job check find jobs for you).
  2. The agents work: one reads the page, one works out what the job wants, one finds your matching experience, one writes, one checks every line against what you saved, and one builds the PDF.
  3. You get the resume in Telegram, and (optionally) a copy in your own Google Drive and a row in your own Google Sheet.

What it uses your Google account for

Only if you connect it, and only your own account: Internify saves each finished resume PDF to a Google Drive folder you choose, and adds or updates one row per application in a Google Sheet you choose, so you can keep track of where you have applied. It does nothing else with your Google data. The files stay private to your account.

Internify's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

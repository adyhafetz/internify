Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Internify is a personal, open-source resume assistant run by its owner for their own use. This policy explains what data it handles.

What Internify accesses

  • Google Drive: it uploads the resume PDFs it creates into the folder you choose.
  • Google Sheets: it adds and updates rows (company, job title, job link, status, résumé link) in the tracker sheet you choose.
  • Telegram: it receives the job links you send and replies with your resume.
  • Job posting text: it reads the public web page of the job link you send.
  • Your saved experience: the education, experience, projects and skills you enter yourself.

How the data is used

The data is used only to write, check, save and track your resumes. To write them, the text of the job posting and your saved experience are sent to the OpenAI API for processing. Nothing is used for advertising, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Where the data is stored

Your saved experience and application history are stored in files on the computer or server where Internify runs, in your own Google Drive and Sheet, and in your Telegram chat. Google access tokens are kept in the owner's private configuration file and are never published.

Google user data

Internify's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the Drive and Sheets features described above. It is not transferred to others, and not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete resumes and rows in your own Drive and Sheet at any time, remove entries with the bot's delete commands, and revoke Internify's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
